# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 5, 2026

FamilyChronica respects the privacy of your family history. This Privacy Policy applies to the FamilyChronica website, private family workspace, applications, support channels, subscriptions, and related digital services. “FamilyChronica,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the operator of these services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. Third-party services may also have their own privacy policies.

## 1. Scope and who is responsible

This policy covers personal information processed when you browse our public pages, create or access an account, build a family archive, upload media, use AI-assisted tools, contact support, join a family by invitation, request a printed product, or otherwise interact with FamilyChronica.

The person who creates or manages a family space controls invitations and many sharing choices inside that space. Family members may also control the memories and profile details they contribute. If another person invited you, their actions and your family’s internal decisions are separate from FamilyChronica’s operation of the service.

## 2. Information we collect

Information you provide

- Account and contact details: name, email address, family name, sign-in and verification records, language, accessibility preferences, and support communications.
- Family content: stories, notes, interview answers, transcripts, translations, photographs, audio, video, captions, dates, places, relationships, family-tree details, and the people connected with a memory.
- AI feature inputs and results: When you use an AI-assisted feature, we may process text you enter or select, story and conversation context, names and relationships, transcripts, recordings or live speech, selected photographs and cropped images of faces, and resulting transcripts, descriptions, suggestions or drafts. We collect these from content you enter, record, select, import or otherwise provide when using the relevant feature.

- Collaboration information: invitations, family roles, membership, sharing settings, review decisions, contributions, and activity connected with a family space.
- Purchase information: plan, order, delivery, billing status, product choices, and transaction references. Payment card details may be collected directly by a payment processor rather than stored by FamilyChronica.
- Requests and feedback: messages sent to support, account recovery details, privacy requests, survey responses, and product feedback.

Information collected automatically

- IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referral page, pages viewed, timestamps, approximate region, language, session information, and error or security events.
- Usage details such as features opened, uploads completed, storage used, AI operations requested, and actions needed to operate, protect, and improve the service.
- App analytics and attribution information: app-instance or installation identifiers, app and operating-system versions, device model, language, region, time zone, network type, session identifiers, event identifiers and timestamps, pages visited, feature interactions, and startup or session duration.
- Subscription conversion information: product identifiers, transaction references, free-trial or subscription events, and actual purchase amounts and currencies. Where available to the SDK, attribution information may also include IP-derived information and campaign or referral information associated with an installation or app activation.


Information about other people

Family history naturally includes information about relatives and other people. Before uploading another person’s private recording, image, contact details, or sensitive story, you should have an appropriate basis to do so and respect any request to limit or remove it.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- create accounts, authenticate users, deliver verification codes, and maintain sessions;
- store, organize, display, search, play, translate, and export family content;
- apply family roles, invitations, sharing choices, and access controls;
- provide transcription, voice conversation, writing assistance, family tree suggestions, photo analysis and restoration, media organization, and other features you request;
- process subscriptions, orders, printed products, support requests, and account recovery;
- detect abuse, prevent unauthorized access, investigate incidents, enforce limits, and protect the service;
- diagnose errors, measure reliability, understand feature performance, and improve accessibility and usability;
- measure app installations, campaign attribution, free-trial starts and paid subscription conversions, evaluate advertising effectiveness, and support campaign optimization;

- communicate service notices, policy updates, security alerts, and—where permitted—product news; and
- comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our agreement with you, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent for future processing when consent is the applicable basis.

## 4. AI-assisted features

FamilyChronica offers optional AI-assisted features, including voice transcription and conversation, story drafting, family tree suggestions, photo analysis and photo restoration. To provide these features, we may send information needed for the relevant request to the third-party AI providers listed below. Depending on the feature, this may include story and conversation text, names and family relationships, transcripts, recordings or live speech, selected photographs and cropped images of faces, and relevant instructions or conversation context.

We collect this information when you enter or select content, record or speak, import photographs, or use the relevant story, photo or family tree feature. For example, when face analysis is enabled for imported photos, cropped images of detected faces may be sent for analysis. Family content may include information about other people; please ensure you have an appropriate basis to share it.

Our AI providers include:

- **DeepSeek:** may process text and photographs, including face crops, for writing assistance, family tree suggestions and photo analysis.
- **OpenAI:** may process text, photographs and voice for AI assistance, conversation, transcription, speech generation and photo restoration.
- **Deepgram:** may process recordings or live speech for transcription and text for speech generation.
- **ByteDance's Volcengine/Doubao:** may process voice and text for voice conversation and speech generation.

The provider depends on the feature and configuration. If a service is unavailable, an alternative provider listed above may be used. Providers process the information to supply the requested services to us, subject to applicable agreements and privacy, security and retention terms.

In the iOS app, before personal information is shared with these third-party AI providers, we explain what information may be shared and with whom and ask for your permission. You may decline; features that require third-party AI sharing will not be available without permission. You can withdraw permission for future AI requests at any time under **Settings → AI Data Sharing**. Withdrawing permission does not automatically delete information already sent. Contact us using the details below to make a privacy request.

AI output may be incomplete or inaccurate. Review names, dates, relationships, quotations, translations and historical facts before saving or sharing them. Where supported, source material is retained so you can compare it with an AI-assisted result.

We do not use private family content to train our own general-purpose AI models. We do not authorize an AI provider to use private family content to train public models unless we clearly disclose the change and obtain any consent required by law.

## 5. Cookies, local storage, and similar technology

We use essential cookies or comparable storage to keep you signed in, protect account sessions, remember language and text-size choices, preserve unfinished selections, and support security. Browser storage may also retain non-sensitive interface preferences and product configuration choices on your device.

We do not send private family stories, photographs, recordings, conversation text or face crops as advertising or attribution event parameters. We use app analytics and advertising attribution SDKs as described below. These SDKs use identifiers, event information and local storage to measure use and conversions. We do not sell personal information for money. The treatment of disclosures for advertising measurement or optimization as “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising may depend on applicable law and the recipient’s processing. You may contact us to exercise applicable privacy rights.

### App analytics and advertising attribution

The iOS app integrates the following services:

- **Google Firebase Analytics:** receives app analytics events and related app-instance or installation identifiers, device and app information, locale and time zone, network type, session information, page visits, feature interactions and subscription operation results. We use it to understand feature use, measure reliability and improve the app. JKCLTelemetry is the app’s event reporting layer that sends these events to Firebase.
- **Meta (Facebook) App Events:** receives app activation and installation-related signals, free-trial and subscription events, and paid purchase events. Purchase events may include product identifiers, actual transaction amounts and currencies. We use these signals to measure and optimize Meta advertising campaigns.
- **AppsFlyer:** receives installation and app activation signals, device and app information, attribution signals, free-trial and subscription events, product and transaction identifiers, and paid purchase amounts and currencies. We use it to attribute installations and conversions to campaigns and measure advertising performance.

The current iOS configuration disables advertising identifier (IDFA) collection in the integrated analytics and attribution SDKs. This does not disable all analytics or attribution processing. These services may still process app-instance or vendor identifiers, IP-related information and event data in accordance with their SDK behavior, configuration and applicable terms.

We do not intentionally include account email addresses, family member names, private stories, photos, audio, video or AI conversation content in analytics or attribution events. AI feature processing is separate and is described in section 4.

A free-trial start is recorded separately from a paid purchase. Paid conversion reporting uses verified App Store transactions and, where available, actual transaction amounts and currencies. The app may identify the first paid renewal after a free trial when it receives an updated transaction or when the user next opens the app. Transaction references are used to avoid duplicate reporting.

These providers may process information on servers outside your country. Their processing and retention are governed by applicable agreements, settings and their privacy information:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
- AppsFlyer: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/

For privacy choices or requests concerning app analytics or advertising attribution, contact support@familychronica.com. Available rights and any required consent depend on applicable law. Withdrawing AI Data Sharing permission controls future AI requests and does not turn off analytics or attribution SDKs.


You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or reset saved preferences.

- With service providers: hosting, storage, email delivery, AI processing, security, customer support, payment, analytics, printing, and shipping providers may process information for services they perform for us. Our AI providers, the types of content they may receive and related choices are described in section 4.
- For legal and safety reasons: we may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or defend legal claims.
- During a business transaction: information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice where required.
- With your direction or consent: we may share information for another purpose that you clearly request or approve.

We require third-party service providers that process personal information on our behalf, including AI service providers, to protect that information with safeguards that are the same as or equivalent to those described in this Privacy Policy. They may process personal information only to provide the services requested by FamilyChronica and in accordance with applicable agreements and privacy laws.

## 7. Family collaboration and sensitive stories

The owner or administrator of a family space may invite members, assign roles, change access, and manage shared content. Contributors should use privacy controls thoughtfully, especially for health history, adoption, financial matters, immigration history, precise locations, information about minors, and memories involving living people who may expect confidentiality.

Removing a person from a family space limits future access through that account but may not remove copies previously exported, downloaded, printed, or independently retained by another authorized family member. FamilyChronica cannot control what an authorized recipient does outside the service.

Historical and genealogical records may include information about deceased people. Privacy laws do not treat this information uniformly, but we ask families to handle it respectfully and consider the interests of living relatives.

## 8. Data retention

We retain account information and family content while an account is active and as needed to provide the service. Retention periods vary by data type, user choices, backup cycles, security needs, transaction records, unresolved disputes, and legal requirements.

When deletion is requested or an account is closed, information is removed from active systems according to our deletion process, except where we need to retain limited records for legal, fraud-prevention, security, billing, or dispute purposes. Residual encrypted backup copies may remain until the relevant backup is securely overwritten. Content shared with or exported by other family members may remain in their possession.

Third-party AI providers may retain information submitted to them according to their applicable service agreements and retention arrangements. Withdrawing permission stops future sharing from the iOS app but does not automatically delete information previously sent. Contact us as described in section 15 to make a privacy request.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. These may include encrypted network connections, access controls, limited administrative permissions, secure session handling, rate limits, monitoring, backups, and procedures for responding to suspected incidents.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your email account and devices, use trusted networks, review family access regularly, and contact us promptly if you believe an account or invitation has been misused. More information is available in our Privacy & Trust Center.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to personal information, obtain a portable copy, correct inaccurate information, delete information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision about a privacy request.

You can manage many choices inside your account and family workspace. For other requests, email support@familychronica.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line. We may verify your identity, confirm authority over a family space, or ask for information needed to locate the relevant records. An authorized agent may submit a request where allowed by law, but we may require proof of authorization and direct verification with the individual.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable privacy right. Some requests may be limited by legal exceptions, the rights of other people, recordkeeping duties, or the need to protect account security.

In the iOS app, you can withdraw permission for future sharing with third-party AI providers under **Settings → AI Data Sharing**. You can decline this sharing and continue to use features that do not require it.

## 11. Children and family content

FamilyChronica is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 may not create their own accounts. If we learn that we collected personal information directly from a child under 13 without required parental consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Adults may preserve family stories and media that include children. The account holder is responsible for using appropriate permissions, avoiding unnecessary sensitive details, and choosing who may view that content. If you believe a child’s information was added inappropriately, contact us.
